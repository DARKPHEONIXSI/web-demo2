About On Ice

A practical home for better skating days.

On Ice brings coaching notes, technique breakdowns, official rules, rink moments, and curated gear into one calm, useful place.

Why this exists

The work between the highlights matters.

Figure skating is often shown through medals, scores, and big moments. On Ice focuses on the quieter parts too: the edge drills, the early sessions, the worn laces, the coaching corrections, and the small breakthroughs that make the performance possible.

What began as training notes has grown into a journal, technique library, official rules reference, visual gallery, and shop for gear that earns its place at the rink.
