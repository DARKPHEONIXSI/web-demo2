{{ settings.get('blog_name', 'On Ice') }} is a journal for skaters who chase the quiet moments between jumps — the early mornings, the worn-out laces, the small breakthroughs nobody else saw happen.

Why this exists

Figure skating is usually told through medals and scores. We wanted somewhere for the rest of it — the technique breakdowns, the gear that actually holds up, the rules nobody explains clearly, and the everyday discipline that never makes the highlight reel.

What began as personal training notes grew into a full journal: coaching write-ups, an ISU rules reference, a curated shop, and a gallery of the sport at its most honest.
